# BAROWO GEO Playbook

Date: 2026-04-17

This document is for GEO in the practical sense of:

- Generative Engine Optimization
- AI discovery readiness
- machine-readable clarity for Google Search, AI Overviews, AI assistants and answer engines

## Goal

Make BAROWO easy to understand, easy to cite and easy to recommend by search systems and AI systems.

## Core Principle

Do not write content for bots.
Write strong, specific, people-first content that machines can clearly parse.

## What BAROWO Should Be Known For

BAROWO should become strongly associated with:

- mobilny bar Trójmiasto
- mobilny bar Gdynia / Gdańsk / Sopot
- drink bar na wesele
- mobilny bar na urodziny
- mobilny bar na event firmowy
- premium bar service with complete setup

## GEO Requirements for BAROWO

### 1. Clear entity consistency

Keep these details consistent everywhere:

- brand name: BAROWO
- primary website: `https://barowo.com/`
- main email
- main phone numbers
- service area
- base location

This consistency should match across:

- homepage
- schema
- Google Business Profile
- Instagram / TikTok bios
- article bylines / publisher info

### 2. Strong homepage machine readability

Homepage should clearly state:

- what BAROWO is
- who it serves
- where it serves
- what event types it supports
- what differentiates the offer
- how to contact and book

Machines should be able to extract this without guessing.

### 3. Service pages or service sections with explicit intent

BAROWO should have dedicated, indexable content for:

- mobilny bar na wesele
- mobilny bar na urodziny
- mobilny bar na event firmowy
- mobilny bar Trójmiasto
- mobilny bar Gdynia / Gdańsk / Sopot

These can be landing pages or strong homepage subsections first, but dedicated pages will scale better.

### 4. People-first, answer-ready content

AI systems favor content that answers specific questions cleanly.

BAROWO articles should keep targeting questions like:

- ile kosztuje mobilny bar w Trójmieście
- czy mobilny bar opłaca się na wesele
- jak dobrać pakiet baru do liczby gości
- co obejmuje mobilny bar na event firmowy

Best format:

- one clear question
- one direct answer early
- then practical detail

### 5. Structured data that matches visible content

Use schema to reinforce what is already visible:

- `LocalBusiness` on the homepage
- `BlogPosting` on articles
- `FAQPage` only if the FAQ is visibly on-page

Do not add schema that claims things not visible on the page.

### 6. Internal linking for intent clustering

Each article should link to:

- homepage / booking section
- the most relevant service intent
- 1–2 related articles

This helps both search crawlers and AI systems understand topic clusters.

## Recommended Content Structure

### Homepage

Must clearly include:

- service category
- city/service area
- event types
- package framing
- trust signals
- contact methods

### Articles

Each article should include:

- strong title
- concise summary paragraph near the top
- visible author/publisher identity
- date published / updated
- internal CTA to BAROWO inquiry

### Future FAQ blocks

High-value FAQ ideas:

- Ile kosztuje mobilny bar na wesele?
- Ile drinków potrzeba na 50 / 100 gości?
- Czy zapewniacie alkohol?
- Jak wcześnie trzeba rezerwować termin?
- Czy dojeżdżacie poza Trójmiasto?

## GEO Content Rules

### Do

- use concrete cities, event types and service language
- answer real booking questions directly
- keep copy specific, not generic
- use visible proof and operational detail
- update old content instead of only adding new content

### Do not

- write vague “AI bait” summaries
- keyword-stuff pages
- generate shallow near-duplicate city pages
- add fake FAQ schema or fake review schema

## BAROWO GEO Roadmap

### Stage 1 — Foundation

1. Complete homepage `LocalBusiness` schema
2. Add article `datePublished` and `dateModified`
3. Add stronger OG/social metadata
4. Keep sitemap freshness accurate

### Stage 2 — Intent coverage

1. Create or strengthen landing content for wedding, birthday and corporate intent
2. Build local relevance for Gdynia, Gdańsk, Sopot, Trójmiasto
3. Add clearer CTA paths from articles to inquiry form

### Stage 3 — Authority signals

1. Add visible publisher/about block
2. Link brand social profiles in schema and site
3. Build references from local directories, partners and venues

## How to Measure GEO

Track:

- Search Console impressions and clicks
- branded queries vs non-branded queries
- article-to-lead conversion
- traffic to local/service-intent pages
- whether AI/search summaries cite BAROWO pages more often over time
